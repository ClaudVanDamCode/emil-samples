Responsive newsletter template, built for pasting into Mailchimp

A sample of the kind of hand-coded email I would build from your PSD. The same HTML file is shown below at desktop width (600 px content) and at phone width (375 px). The brand and content are made up for this sample; the grey blocks stand where your PSD images go once they are exported and uploaded to Mailchimp's Content Studio.

  • Table-based layout with inline styles, so it holds together in Gmail, Outlook (desktop + web), Apple Mail and Yahoo.
  • Two-column product row and footer stack into one column on phones (media query plus fluid fallback for clients that ignore media queries).
  • Outlook "ghost tables" (MSO conditionals) keep the 600 px width in Outlook for Windows; bulletproof buttons work without images.
  • Mailchimp-ready: mc:edit regions for text and images, preheader text, and the required merge tags (*|UNSUB|*, *|UPDATE_PROFILE|*, *|LIST:ADDRESSLINE|*).
  • Paste into Mailchimp via "Code your own → Paste in code"; editable blocks stay editable for your team.
