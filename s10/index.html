What the real bot does

  • Catalog of up to 30 products in categories, each with photo, description, price and live stock.
  • Cart with + / - per item, totals, and stock checks so nobody can order more than you have.
  • Checkout asks for name, phone and delivery or pick-up, then sends a Telegram invoice. Payment goes through Telegram Payments with the provider you connect in BotFather (for example Stripe), so card data never touches the bot.
  • On successful payment the order is saved, stock is reduced, the customer gets a receipt and the owner gets a message with the order.
  • Owner commands in Telegram: stock, setstock, orders, ship 1001 (tells the customer the order is on its way), low-stock alerts, CSV export of orders.
  • Products can be edited from a simple spreadsheet (CSV) or with owner commands, no code changes.
  • Python with aiogram 3 and SQLite; runs on any small VPS or a free-tier host, with a start script and a short setup guide.

How the code is laid out

shopbot/
  bot.py            start, routers, polling or webhook
  catalog.py        categories, product cards, cart buttons
  checkout.py       name / phone / delivery steps, invoice, payment
  owner.py          stock, setstock, orders, ship commands, alerts, CSV
  db.py             SQLite: products, carts, orders, order_items
  products.csv      your 30 products (edit in Excel)
  .env.example      BOT_TOKEN, PAYMENT_TOKEN, OWNER_ID, CURRENCY
  README.md         setup in 10 minutes
