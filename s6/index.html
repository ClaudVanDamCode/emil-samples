Walk-through from rendered 360 images

Drag to look around. Click a ring on the floor (or the buttons) to move to the next point. The image you see is only the pre-rendered panoramas: the 3D model is used as an invisible surface they are projected onto, so walls and furniture keep their place while you move and the two renders blend.

The demo panoramas are rendered in this page from a simple test room, standing in for your offline renders.
