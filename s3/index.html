Indexing recovery audit after a URL change and a domain migration

How I find out why most pages of a migrated site sit in "Crawled – currently not indexed", and what the fix list looks like. The case below mirrors a common path: a URL structure change (a new /en1/ prefix), then a move to a new domain with 301s and the Change of Address tool.

4. Why pages that ranked for years get deindexed after a move

Mixed signals

Redirect says "the new URL", canonical says "the old URL", sitemap lists a third variant. Google picks its own canonical and often leaves the rest out.

Thin and duplicate sets

Tag, archive, paginated and parameter pages multiply after a URL change. They dilute the site and raise the share Google decides not to index.

Content quality after a core update

When the drop started with a core update, technical fixes alone do not bring it back. The audit separates the technical share from the content share so you know which work pays.

Lost link equity

Backlinks that end in a 404, a home-page redirect or a 302 do not pass their value. Those are the cheapest wins to recover.
